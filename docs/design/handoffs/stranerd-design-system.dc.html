01

Color

Three semantic families, kept strictly separate so a learner can always tell anatomy from system feedback from AI output. Blue is the product and every deterministic action. Violet is exclusively Mentor and Voice — it never appears on a control the learner operates manually. Green, amber and red carry state only.

Rule

Violet is reserved. If a control spends credits or renders model output, it is violet; if it does not, it is blue or neutral — no exceptions. This is how a learner tells deterministic Lab validation apart from AI guidance without reading a label (§10, §21).

Migration

The names above land in shadcn.css as it is actually written: --muted-ui, --border-ui, --accent-ui. Today's primary is a neutral oklch(0.205 0 0) in light and #8eb4ff in dark — two unrelated identities. One blue replaces both. --radius stays 0.625rem; --font-sans moves off Geist. The duplicate .dark block and the four other stylesheets are deleted, not overridden.

02

Typography

Three faces with one job each. Instrument Sans runs the entire product interface. Instrument Serif appears only on marketing and section openers, giving the landing page editorial voice without a second product identity. IBM Plex Mono carries quantities — credits, timers, question counts, file sizes, structure coordinates — so numbers stay tabular and never reflow.
