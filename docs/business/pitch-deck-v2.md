## Slide 1 — Title

**Stranerd**

Hands-on STEM and medical training, without the physical lab.

We let students take apart the real machine, body, or circuit on their screen — guided one-on-one by an AI mentor.

---

## Slide 2 — The Problem

Imagine learning to perform surgery, repair an industrial generator, or build a circuit board only by reading about it. That's how millions of STEM and medical students across Africa graduate every year.

Physical equipment is expensive, so education stays stuck in theory. Universities teach the definitions; employers need people who can do the work. Graduates enter high-stakes fields having never touched the tools of their trade.

---

## Slide 3 — The Solution

Stranerd closes the gap between theory and real-world skill. Instead of recreating a lab room, we simulate the actual thing students need to master — the machine, the circuit, the human body, the system — and put an AI mentor beside them.

- **Real components, not diagrams:** Students take apart generators and engines, build circuits, and work through anatomy directly on their device or in AR.
- **An AI mentor for every topic:** A subject-specific AI guide teaches one-on-one, grades precision, and shows the real consequence of each mistake as it happens.
- **Practice without risk:** We assess real performance — accuracy, speed, decisions — not just multiple-choice recall, so students reach true mastery before touching real hardware.

---

## Slide 4 — How It's Built (Why It's Trustworthy)

Two engines work together:

- **A correctness engine** computes the ground truth. Real physics, math, and medical models decide whether a circuit works, whether the right artery was isolated, what happens when a step is wrong. This can't be guessed or made up.
- **An AI teaching layer** turns that truth into a lesson — explaining what happened, mentoring the student, and adapting to them.

Our AI mentors are grounded in licensed, full-text medical and engineering literature (via Valyu), so they teach from authoritative sources, not scraped web text. Content is also verified by partner-school experts, lecturers, and textbooks.

---

## Slide 5 — Why Now

- **Labs barely exist:** Over 90% of African schools lack a properly equipped science laboratory. Building and maintaining them is too expensive.
- **Skills don't match jobs:** An estimated 83% of young people entering Africa's labor market struggle to find work, largely from a skills mismatch — they know the theory but can't do the job.
- **A massive engineer shortage:** Africa needs an estimated 2.5 million more engineers to meet its development needs, and universities can't scale training without equipment.

## Slide 7 — How It Works

1. **Open a module.** The student picks a topic — say anatomy or electrical circuits — and an accurate, interactive 3D model appears on their device.
2. **Learn with the AI mentor.** A guide for that topic walks them through each part and what it does as they interact with it.
3. **Prove it with a real task.** Instead of a text quiz, they get a live challenge — "fix this short circuit," "isolate this artery." The mentor tracks their steps, speed, and accuracy and gives real feedback.

---

## Slide 8 — Who We Serve

**We start with students, then scale to institutions.**

- **Students first (B2C):** Ambitious science, engineering, and medical students who need real practice to pass exams and prepare for work — and who, in markets like Nigeria, take their learning into their own hands rather than wait for their schools. This is our entry point: fast adoption, direct payment, immediate feedback on what works.

- **Institutions next (B2B):** Once students on a campus are already using Stranerd, we sell schools a license to give every student access — using proven student demand as our wedge into slow institutional procurement. Schools get world-class practical training without spending millions on physical labs.

## Slide 9 — Business Model

A subscription model built for the African market, all running on one unit — **credits**. One credit unlocks one practical simulation.

- **Student subscriptions (primary):** A low flat monthly fee with included credits. Built for students paying out of pocket.
- **Pay-as-you-go credits (primary):** For students who can't commit monthly — buy a few credits to unlock a single practical, like one generator-repair module. Lowest barrier to entry.
- **Institutional licenses (scale):** Per-student-per-month pricing sold to universities by student volume, with a monthly credit allocation per student. This is our expansion motion once campus adoption is proven.
